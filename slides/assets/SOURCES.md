# Brand assets

Exa, Mem0, and Qwen artwork is reused from the original AIE presentation’s local brand assets. The logos retain their respective owners’ rights and are not covered by the application’s MIT license.

- Exa: original white SVG wordmark from the AIE deck.
- Mem0: original SVG from the AIE architecture diagram.
- Qwen: original PNG from the AIE architecture diagram.

The Robin pricing excerpt is typeset from the official [San Francisco page](https://www.robinomakase.com/location/robin-san-francisco/), checked November 6, 2026. Its facts, provenance, and limits are recorded in [evidence.json](../evidence.json). It is not a screenshot or a captured tool response. The phone conversation is authored teaching content and is labeled separately from live mode. No stock photography is used.

Similarweb, Financial Datasets, and Particle artwork is reused from the original AIE Exa Connect assets. They appear as examples in the Exa Agent/Connect access path, documented in [Exa Connect](https://exa.ai/docs/agent/connect/overview). Their respective owners retain rights to the artwork.

The highlights slide uses [exa-search-capture.json](../exa-search-capture.json), a selected result from a real Search request. Its provenance differs from the authored phone conversation.
